Sound Shadow

Elephant & Castle · London SE1

Road noise on every floor of every facade. Bright walls face the traffic. Dark walls are in the sound shadow of another building, or of their own.

  • The ground shows the official 2022 road noise map, calculated 4 m above the street.
  • The walls show a model trained on that map. It was tested on streets it never saw and is off by 2.8 dB on average. Above the 2nd floor nothing official exists to check it against.
  • Only road traffic counts. The railway viaduct is not included.
